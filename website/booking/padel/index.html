In detail

Padel and sports courts

Built for courts, not appointments

A court is a resource, not a person, and each sport has its own courts and durations. You set up padel, tennis or football with 60 or 90 minute options and their prices, and each sport stays on its own courts.

Instant confirmation

Bookings confirm straight away with no staff approval, and the player gets the court, time, price and your location with a map link. Evening peaks stop depending on who's at the front desk.

  • Free courts checked live
  • A court is held while the player decides
  • Confirmation with address and map link

Your own courts, your own channel

Players book directly on your WhatsApp number, and every conversation and booking stays with your business. You can use ordr.ae alongside your own website, and you get a public page at yourname.ordr.ae that shows your courts and prices.

A calendar for the front desk

See every court on one calendar, on a laptop or phone. Staff can read any conversation and step in, for example when a player asks about coaching or a tournament that isn't in your catalog.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Does it work for tennis and football too?
Yes. It handles padel, tennis and football courts, each with its own durations, prices and courts.
Can I charge different prices at peak times?
Not yet. Each sport and duration has one price for now.
Can players split the payment or pay a deposit?
Not yet. Online payment isn't available, so players pay at your venue.
Does it support recurring weekly bookings?
Not yet. Each booking is made on its own.
