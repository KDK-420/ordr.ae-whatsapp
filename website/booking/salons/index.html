In detail

Salons and spas

Stop answering "are you free at 4?" all day

Your receptionist and stylists can't reply while they're working with a client, and those messages pile up in the evening. The assistant answers each one straight away with services, prices and free times, so a client who messages at 22:00 is booked before they ask a competitor.

Services, stylists and times

Set up your services, durations and team once. Each stylist only gets the services they do, and the assistant works out real free times from your calendar, with the buffers you choose between appointments.

  • Clients choose a stylist, or take the first free one
  • Book, move and cancel in the chat
  • Reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before

Remembers regulars, with consent

If a client agrees, the assistant remembers their favourite stylist, so the next booking takes one message. It only remembers after a yes, and forgets on request.

Arabic and English in one chat

Clients can write or send voice notes in either language and get an answer in the same one. They can also ask for photos of your work or your price list, and the assistant shares what you've given it.

Your salon's own page

You also get a public page at yourname.ordr.ae with your services and prices, in both languages. Prices update when your catalog does.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Does it work for spas, barbershops and nail bars?
Yes. It's built for any business that books services with staff: salons, spas, barbershops and nail bars.
Can I use my salon's existing WhatsApp number?
Yes, as long as it moves to the WhatsApp Business Platform, which means it stops working in the regular WhatsApp app on a phone. Your team then replies from the dashboard. You can also use a new number.
What if a client wants something unusual?
If the answer isn't in your details, the assistant says so and alerts your team, who reply from the inbox with the whole conversation in front of them.
Does it replace my point-of-sale system?
No. ordr.ae handles conversations, bookings and your calendar. It doesn't take payment, so you keep your till as it is.
