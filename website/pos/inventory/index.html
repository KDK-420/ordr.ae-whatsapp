In detail

Inventory

Recipes do the counting

Tell ordr what one latte uses: 18 g of beans, 220 ml of milk, a cup and a lid. From then on every latte sold takes exactly that out of stock, whether it was sold at the till, on your website or on WhatsApp.

Sizes, extras and takeaway

A large size uses more milk and the larger cup. An extra shot uses more beans. A drink for here uses no paper cup at all. Recipes follow the choices the customer made.

  • Ingredients by the gram and the millilitre
  • Cups, lids, bags and boxes by the piece
  • Stock comes back when an order is cancelled

See what needs buying

Every item has a reorder level. The inventory screen shows what is running low, what has run out, and the value of everything you hold.

Purchases and suppliers

Record an order to a supplier, starting from the items that are low. When the delivery arrives, receive it with one tap and the stock goes up.

Counts, wastage and corrections

Count the store room and enter what you found; the system records the difference. Log a spilled carton or expired pastries as wastage, and it appears in the report as a cost.

What your stock costs you

The usage report shows what was used, wasted and bought over a day, a week or a month, with the cost of each item.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Do I have to set up a recipe for every item?
Only for the items whose stock you want to track. An item without a recipe still sells. It just doesn't change stock, and the Recipes screen flags it.
Will it stop a sale when stock runs out?
No. A sale is never blocked. The level goes below zero and shows as out of stock, which tells you a count or a purchase is missing.
Can it track cups, lids and packaging?
Yes. Packaging is counted by the piece, and a recipe can use it for takeaway orders only.
Does it work across branches?
Each branch has its own stock levels. Moving stock between branches isn't available yet.
Does it send low-stock alerts?
Not yet. Low and out-of-stock items are listed on the inventory screen.
