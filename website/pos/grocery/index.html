In detail

Grocery stores

Stop copying lists by hand

When orders come in as free-form messages, someone has to read each one, check the shelves, work out the total and reply. The assistant does that first part, so your staff can get on with packing.

Products, sizes and stock

Add your products with their sizes and prices. Customers ask in their own words, and the assistant finds the match, tells them the price, and says so honestly when something is out of stock.

  • Variants such as size or weight
  • Stock checked before it offers an item
  • Delivery or pickup, with your delivery fee

Arabic and English

Customers can write or send a voice note in either language, and they get an answer in the same one.

Keep your regulars on your number

Orders come straight to your own WhatsApp number and your orders board, and your team can step into any chat.
