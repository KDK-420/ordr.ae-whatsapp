A kitchen display for every order, from every channel.

Replace paper tickets with a screen. Orders appear the second they are placed, each station sees its own items, and nothing gets lost under the pass.

Book a demo →▶ See a live store

Works on a tablet, a laptop or a wall screen.

In detail

Kitchen display

Four columns, one glance

New, Preparing, Ready and Completed. One tap moves a ticket along. Each ticket shows the order number, whether it is dine-in, takeaway or delivery, the table or the customer's name, and every item with its size, extras and notes.

A screen for each station

Tell ordr which categories belong to the bar and which to the bakery or the grill. Each station's screen shows only its own items, and the pass sees the whole order.

  • Drinks to the bar, food to the kitchen
  • Tap a single item when it is done
  • Add or rename stations yourself

A clock on every ticket

Each ticket shows how long it has been waiting. It turns amber after five minutes and red after ten, so the slow one stands out. The bottom of the screen shows today's orders, the average preparation time and how many are still waiting.

All your channels on one screen

A sale at the till, an order from your website and an order placed on WhatsApp become the same kind of ticket. Customers who ordered online see their order move to Preparing and Ready on their own phone.

A sound for new orders

Switch on the chime and the screen rings when a new ticket arrives. Go full screen on a wall display or on a tablet in a stand.

What it runs on

Any screen with a browser: a tablet, an old laptop, or a TV with a small computer behind it. It updates live. If the connection drops, the tickets on screen stay there and it catches up by itself when the connection returns.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Do I need a special kitchen screen?
No. Any tablet, laptop or TV with a modern browser works. Put it where the cooks can see it.
Can I still print kitchen tickets?
Not yet. Kitchen printing is planned. Today the kitchen works from the screen.
Does it work without the ordr POS till?
Yes. It can show orders from your ordering website and WhatsApp on their own. It works best with the till, because then walk-in orders appear too.
How many screens can I have?
There is no fixed limit. Open the display on each screen and choose the station it should show.
What happens if the internet drops?
Tickets already on screen stay there. New ones arrive when the connection returns.
