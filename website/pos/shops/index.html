In detail

Shops

A shop assistant on your WhatsApp

Selling through chat works, but only while someone is there to reply. The assistant answers product questions at any hour, suggests what fits, and tells customers honestly when something is out of stock.

Products, variants and live stock

Add products with variants such as size or weight, prices and stock. The assistant checks stock before it offers anything, so customers aren't promised something you've already sold.

  • Suggests products with prices
  • Checks live stock
  • Builds the cart in the chat

From question to order

Customers build a cart and choose delivery or pickup. The order lands in your dashboard for your team to prepare, and the customer gets updates as it moves.

A page for your shop

You also get a public page at yourname.ordr.ae that shows your products and prices in Arabic and English, and links customers straight to the chat.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Does it connect to Shopify or my website?
Not today. You keep your products and stock in ordr.ae.
Can customers pay by card in the chat?
Not yet. Customers pay on delivery, at pickup, or however you take payment today.
Is this the same as the WhatsApp catalog?
No. The catalog in the WhatsApp Business app is a list of items. ordr.ae has a conversation, checks stock and builds the order.
