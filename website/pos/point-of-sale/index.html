In detail

Point of sale

Fast at the counter

Your menu is a grid of photos with category tabs and a search box. Tap an item, pick the size or an extra, and add a note for the kitchen. The total with VAT is always on screen.

  • Dine-in with a table number, takeaway or delivery
  • Sizes, milks and extras, each with its price
  • The customer's name and mobile number on the order

Cash, card, or pay later

Take cash and the till works out the change. Take a card on your own card machine and record it on the sale. For a table that pays at the end, send the order to the kitchen now and take the payment later.

Hold an order

A customer steps away to find their wallet. Hold the order, serve the next person, and bring it back with one tap.

The day in numbers

See sales, VAT, the average order and what is still unpaid. The report splits the day by cash and card, by counter, website and WhatsApp, and by dine-in, takeaway and delivery.

Every order in one list

Orders from the counter, your website and WhatsApp sit in the same list, with their status and whether they are paid. Open one to take payment or to cancel it. A cancelled order puts its stock back.

What it doesn't do yet

It doesn't print receipts, open a cash drawer or work without an internet connection, and it doesn't talk to a card machine. We would rather you know before the demo than after.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

What do I need to run it?
A tablet or laptop with a modern browser and an internet connection. Nothing is installed.
Can it print receipts?
Not yet. Receipt and kitchen printing are planned. Today the kitchen works from the kitchen display.
Does it handle VAT?
Yes. Choose whether your prices include the 5% VAT or have it added on top, and every order shows the VAT amount. It doesn't issue tax invoices or file returns.
Can several staff use it at once?
Yes. Each person signs in on their own device.
Can I change my menu myself?
Yes. Add items, photos, prices, sizes and extras under Products. The change shows on the till, the website and WhatsApp.
