In detail

Restaurants and cafés

Why order on WhatsApp

Phone orders tie up staff during the rush, and messages get missed when the kitchen is busy. On WhatsApp the customer's order arrives complete and written down, and the conversation stays on your number with your own customers.

From menu to kitchen

Customers browse categories and items in the chat, see prices, and build a cart. At checkout they choose delivery or pickup. New orders appear on your orders board, where your team accepts them and moves them along.

  • Menu and prices from your catalog
  • Delivery or pickup, with your delivery fee
  • VAT shown the way you set it

Updates at each step

Customers get a message as their order moves from accepted to ready or out for delivery, so fewer people call to ask where their food is. They can also ask for the status in the chat.

Arabic and English menus

Your menu appears in the customer's language, and they can switch at any time. If someone asks about allergens or a special request that isn't in your details, the assistant passes it to your team.

Frequently asked

Questions?

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Can it take table reservations?
Not yet. Restaurant ordering covers menus, carts and orders today.
Does it connect to my POS or to delivery apps?
Not today. Orders arrive on the ordr.ae orders board, and your team works from there.
How do customers pay?
On delivery, at pickup, or however you take payment today. Online card payment isn't available yet.
Does it work for cloud kitchens and cafés?
Yes. It's built for restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens.
