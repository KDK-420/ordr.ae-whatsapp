In detail

ordr POS

One order, everywhere it matters

A sale at the till, an order on your website and a message on WhatsApp are the same thing to ordr POS: an order. It appears on the kitchen screen, in the day's sales and in the stock count, without anyone typing it in again.

Start small, add modules later

You don't have to take all five. A café can start with the till and the kitchen screen, add the ordering website next month and stock after that. We switch a module on, and its menu appears for your team.

  • Staff sign in at your restaurant's own address
  • The same colours on your website and your staff screens
  • More than one branch, each with its own stations and stock

Built for the UAE

Prices in dirhams, with the 5% VAT included or added on top, as you choose. WhatsApp ordering answers in Arabic and English. The ordering website and the staff screens are in English today.

What it runs on

Any tablet, laptop or wall screen with a modern browser. There is nothing to install. The screens update live, and the till needs an internet connection to take a sale.

Shops and grocery stores

Shops and grocery stores use WhatsApp ordering today, with a product catalog and stock. The till, the kitchen display and inventory are built for restaurants and cafés first.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Is ordr POS a full point-of-sale system?
For restaurants and cafés, yes. It rings up sales, records cash and card payments, sends orders to the kitchen and counts stock. It doesn't print receipts, open a cash drawer or work without internet yet.
Do I need special hardware?
No. It runs in a browser on tablets, laptops and screens you already have. Card payments are taken on your own card machine and recorded on the sale.
How do customers pay online?
On the ordering website and on WhatsApp, customers pay at pickup or on delivery. Online card payment isn't available yet.
Does it connect to delivery apps such as Talabat?
Not today. ordr POS runs your own channels: the counter, your website and WhatsApp.
Can I use it for more than one branch?
Yes. Each branch has its own orders, kitchen stations and stock, under one account.
What does it cost?
It depends on the modules you take and how many branches you run. Book a demo and we'll give you a clear price.
Can I use it next to ordr Booking?
Yes, talk to us if your business takes both orders and bookings.
