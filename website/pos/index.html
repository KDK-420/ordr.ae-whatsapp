In detail

ordr POS

Your counter, open all day

Customers already message you to ask what you have and how much it costs. ordr POS answers at any hour from your real catalog and turns the chat into an order, so a sale doesn't depend on someone being free to reply.

From chat to order

Customers see your categories, items, variants and prices in the chat. The assistant checks stock, builds the cart and takes the delivery or pickup details.

  • Menu or product catalog in Arabic and English
  • Variants such as size or weight
  • Live stock, so nobody orders what's sold out

One board for every order

New orders land on your orders board, on a laptop or phone. Your team accepts them and moves them along, and customers get an update at each step.

Prices the way you run them

Set your delivery fee and whether your prices include VAT. Change a price in your catalog and the assistant uses it straight away.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Is it a full point-of-sale system?
Not yet. ordr POS handles orders and sales on WhatsApp and the web chat. It doesn't run a till, print receipts or take card payments, so you keep your current till for walk-in customers.
How do customers pay?
On delivery, at pickup, or however you take payment today. Online card payment isn't available yet.
Does it connect to my till or to delivery apps?
Not today. Orders arrive on the ordr.ae orders board, and your team works from there.
Can I use it next to ordr Booking?
Yes, talk to us if your business takes both orders and bookings.
