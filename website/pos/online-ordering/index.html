In detail

Online ordering site

A site with your name on it

Your restaurant gets its own address, such as yourname.ordr.ae, with your menu, your photos and one of five colour themes, or your own colours. It works on a phone without an app.

A menu that sells

Categories, search, photos and clear prices. Customers choose a size and extras, add a note, and see the total with VAT before they order.

Pickup or delivery

Customers choose pickup or delivery, enter their name and mobile number, and place the order. You set the delivery fee. They pay at the counter or on delivery.

Customers follow their order

After ordering, each customer gets a page that shows the order moving from placed to preparing to ready, updated as your kitchen works. Fewer people call to ask where their food is.

Straight to the kitchen

A website order appears on the kitchen display and in the till's order list the moment it is placed, and comes out of stock like any other sale.

Your customers stay yours

The customer's name and number are on the order, in your own system. They appear in your customer list with how often they order and what they have spent.

Frequently asked

Questions?
We have answers.

Straight answers about how this works in practice.

Can customers pay by card online?
Not yet. Today they pay at pickup or on delivery. Online card payment is planned.
Can I use my own domain?
Not yet. Your site lives at an ordr.ae address today. Custom domains are planned.
Is the site in Arabic?
The ordering site is in English today. WhatsApp ordering answers in Arabic and English.
Do you deliver the orders?
No. Your own drivers deliver. ordr shows your team the delivery orders and their addresses.
Does it replace Talabat or Deliveroo?
It doesn't list you on a marketplace. It gives your regulars a way to order from you directly, next to whichever apps you already use.
Can I change the menu and photos myself?
Yes, under Products. Changes show on the site straight away.
