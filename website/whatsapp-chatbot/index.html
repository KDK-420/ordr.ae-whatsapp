More than a menu of buttons

Many WhatsApp bots push people through fixed menus, and customers give up when their question doesn't fit. ordr.ae reads plain sentences instead. Someone can ask three things in one message, change their mind halfway, or send a voice note from the car, and the conversation still moves forward.

Answers come from your data

The assistant works from your catalog, calendar and business details. If the answer isn't there, it says so and asks your team, so it doesn't invent a price or promise a time you don't have.

  • Prices and services from your catalog
  • Free times from your live calendar
  • Address, hours and parking from your business details

Arabic and English, typed or spoken

It follows the language of the customer's latest message, so a chat can switch between Arabic and English without anyone choosing a setting. Voice notes are transcribed and answered, and photos are understood too.

Your team stays in charge

When a customer needs a person, the assistant says so and alerts your staff on WhatsApp and on their phones. It keeps answering everything else in the meantime. Your team can read any conversation in the shared inbox and take over whenever they want.

Built for how you sell

The same assistant works differently for each kind of business: salons and spas, padel and sports courts, resorts and hotels, restaurants and cafés, and shops.

Questions

Good questions.

Does it use the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. ordr.ae connects to the WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. Our guide to the WhatsApp Business API in the UAE explains what that involves.

How is this different from the WhatsApp Business app?

The app gives you quick replies and a greeting for a person to manage on a phone. ordr.ae holds a real conversation, checks availability, completes bookings and orders, and gives your whole team one inbox.

Can it answer at night and at weekends?

Yes. It answers whenever a customer writes. Anything that needs a person waits in your inbox, and the customer is told so.

Can customers pay in the chat?

Not yet. For now customers pay at your venue, on delivery, or however you take payment today. Online card payment is on the way.
