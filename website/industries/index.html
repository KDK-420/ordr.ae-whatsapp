Questions

Good questions.

What's the difference between ordr POS and ordr Booking?

ordr POS is for products: menus, carts, stock, delivery and pickup. ordr Booking is for time: appointments, courts and rooms on a calendar.

My industry isn't listed. Can I still use it?

Probably. If you sell products or time, the same system usually fits. Write to us and we'll tell you honestly.

Is everything on this page ready today?

The businesses in the "ready today" lists are. The "also a good fit" lists run on the same system, but we'll want to look at how you work first.
