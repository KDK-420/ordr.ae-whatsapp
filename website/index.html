What happens while you are busy, or asleep.

An example day at a padel club. Salons, resorts, restaurants and shops work the same way.

  1. 23:48

    A player books after closing.

    They send a voice note asking for a court tomorrow at eight. The assistant checks the calendar, holds the court, and confirms with the address and map link.

  2. 10:15

    Someone asks about parking, in Arabic.

    The answer comes from the details you gave it, in the customer's language. If it doesn't know, it says so.

  3. 14:05

    A customer wants a refund.

    That is for a person. The assistant tells them the team will reply, and your staff get a WhatsApp alert and a notification on their phone with the customer's message.

  4. 18:00

    The reminder goes out by itself.

    24 hours and 2 hours before each booking, with no one lifting a finger.

  5. 20:00

    They arrive, and your calendar already knows.

    Every booking sits on your calendar by person or court. One tap checks them in.

What it will not do

It doesn't guess.

An assistant that talks to your customers has to be one you can trust.

No invented prices

Every price, time and booking comes from your catalog and calendar. If the answer isn't there, it says so and asks your team.

No double bookings

A slot is held while the customer decides, so two people can't take the same one.

No memory without a yes

It only remembers a preference after the customer agrees, and it forgets on request.

Questions

Good questions.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes, as long as it moves to the WhatsApp Business Platform, which means it stops working in the regular WhatsApp app on a phone. Your team then replies from the dashboard. You can also use a new number.

What happens when the assistant can't answer?

It tells the customer and alerts your team on WhatsApp and on their phones. Your staff reply from the inbox with the whole conversation in front of them.

Which languages does it speak?

Arabic and English. It follows the language of the customer's latest message, and that includes voice notes. Other languages aren't supported yet.

Can customers pay online?

Not yet. For now customers pay at your venue, on delivery, or however you take payment today. Online card payment is on the way.

What does it cost?

It depends on your business and how many locations you run. Write to us and we'll give you a clear price.

How is customer data handled?

Each business only sees its own customers. Our privacy policy explains what is stored and who is involved in plain language.
