A quick overview of how ordr can fit into your business.

What is ordr?
ordr is an AI-assisted business platform that helps you respond to customers and handle bookings or orders through WhatsApp.
What is the difference between POS and Booking?
ordr POS is oriented toward products and orders. ordr Booking is designed for appointments, availability and reservations. ordr POS doesn't run a till or take card payments, so you keep your current till for walk-in customers.
Can it answer in Arabic and English?
Yes. The platform is designed to serve customers in both Arabic and English, and it follows the language of the customer's latest message, including voice notes. Other languages aren't supported yet.
What happens when a customer needs a person?
The assistant can hand the conversation over to your team when human assistance is required. It alerts your staff on WhatsApp and on their phones, and they reply from the inbox with the whole conversation in front of them.
Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?
Yes, as long as it moves to the WhatsApp Business Platform, which means it stops working in the regular WhatsApp app on a phone. Your team then replies from the dashboard. You can also use a new number.
Can customers pay online?
Not yet. For now customers pay at your venue, on delivery, or however you take payment today. Online card payment is on the way.
What does it cost?
It depends on your business and how many locations you run. Write to us and we'll give you a clear price.
How is customer data handled?
Each business only sees its own customers. Our privacy policy explains what is stored and who is involved in plain language.
Is the conversation in the phone real?
The phone on this page is an interactive frontend demonstration using sample businesses and sample data. It does not create real orders or bookings.
